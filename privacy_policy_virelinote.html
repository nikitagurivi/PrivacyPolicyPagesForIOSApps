Privacy Policy – VireliNote

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

VireliNote (the “App”) is developed by Gureen AI LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information the App Handles

The App may handle the following information to provide its features:

  • Audio recordings you create or import.
  • Transcripts, summaries, notes, recording dates, and conversation identifiers.
  • App settings, such as audio-retention and summarization preferences.
  • If account features are available and you choose to sign in, your account identifier, display name, email address, sign-in provider, session information, and subscription or feature entitlement status.
  • Technical request information ordinarily sent when connecting to an online service, such as an IP address, device and operating-system information, timestamps, and error or security information.

Audio Recordings

Audio recordings stay on the device where they were recorded or imported. The App does not upload audio recordings to our cloud service or sync them through iCloud. They are also excluded from device backups. Audio can therefore be lost if the device or the App’s data is lost, reset, or erased.

By default, audio is kept for 30 days, or for one year when its conversation is bookmarked. Audio that is never transcribed is deleted after 30 days. You can change available retention periods in the App’s settings. Deleting audio does not automatically delete its transcript, summary, or notes.

Transcripts, Notes, and iCloud

Transcripts, summaries, notes, conversation metadata, and settings may sync between your devices through your private Apple iCloud account when iCloud is available. Apple processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy. Recordings in which the App detects no conversation remain only on the device.

Summaries are generated on your device. Your transcripts, summaries, and notes are not sent to us for AI processing.

Optional Account Features

If account features are offered, they are optional. You may sign in using Apple or Google. The sign-in provider gives the App an identity credential and may provide your name and email address. We use this information to create or access your VireliNote account, maintain your session, protect the service, and determine access to eligible features. We do not receive your Apple or Google password. Signing in does not upload your audio, transcripts, summaries, or notes to us.

How We Use Information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide transcription, summarization, synchronization, account, and support features.
  • Authenticate users and manage sessions and feature eligibility.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and prevent abuse of the App and cloud service.
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information or use your recordings or notes for advertising.

Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate features you choose to use, including Apple for iCloud and Sign in with Apple, Google for Google sign-in, and cloud hosting providers used by our account service. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies or under agreements requiring them to protect it. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

Retention and Deletion

Local audio is retained according to the settings described above. You can delete content in the App. Deleting synced content also initiates deletion from iCloud, although changes may take time to reach your other devices. Deletion does not remove copies you exported, shared, or stored outside the App.

Account and security information is retained only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or logs before it is deleted.

Your Choices and Rights

You can adjust audio-retention settings, delete content, sign out, or stop using the App. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before completing it. Information stored in your iCloud account can also be managed through the App and your Apple account.

Recording Other People

You are responsible for complying with all applicable laws when recording, transcribing, processing, keeping, or sharing a conversation. Before recording a private conversation, notify everyone taking part and obtain all consent required by law. The App cannot determine whether you have permission to record another person.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our account or cloud service. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your device, Apple account, Google account, and VireliNote account credentials.

International Processing

If you use optional online features, information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate. Those countries may have data protection laws different from those where you live.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the “Last Updated” date. When appropriate, we may also provide notice in the App.
